## 📖 Overview
<a id="overview"></a>

**Chrometana** is a revolutionary Google Chrome extension designed to redirect searches from Microsoft's Cortana to your preferred browser. Available exclusively for Windows, this tool seamlessly integrates with your system, allowing you to maintain your browsing history and online activity in Google Chrome. 

With **Chrometana**, you can enjoy a hassle-free browsing experience using your favorite search engines like Google, DuckDuckGo, and Yahoo.
